# Pilot Rubric Anchors

Score each dimension 1-4 from the typed answer sheet (A6) only, never from the chat transcript. The scorer must not know the condition.

Before session 1, have the second scorer and yourself score 2 invented sample answers each and compare. Fix any anchor you disagree on.

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Problem understanding** | Restates the brief or names a solution; no stakeholder or scope | Names a problem and stakeholder but broad or symptom-level | Specific stakeholder, scope and cause, plus why it matters | Also says what is out of scope and why this framing beat another framing |
| **Evidence discipline** | Guesses presented as facts | Mentions some evidence but cannot say which claims are assumptions | Separates at least two assumptions from evidence-backed claims and says where evidence came from | Ranks assumptions by risk, names a cheap test for the riskiest, separates what AI said from what they verified |
| **Idea development** | Generic idea unconnected to the problem | Connected to the problem but ignores constraints (time, data, users) | Fits the problem, names key constraints and one main risk | Also explains how it differs from existing solutions or obvious alternatives |
| **Independent defense** | Cannot explain, or only repeats wording | Explains what the idea is but not why it was chosen | Explains in own words why chosen over at least one alternative | Also says what evidence would change their mind and answers one follow-up challenge coherently |
