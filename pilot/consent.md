# Participant Consent Script

*(Read aloud to each participant before the session, then get an explicit verbal "yes")*

> "I am testing a tool that helps people think through hackathon problems. You will do two 45-minute sessions with an AI tool, then answer a few questions with the AI closed. I will record how long things take and what you type into the tools and the answer form. Your answers are scored anonymously and I will not share your idea or name. You can stop at any time and ask me to delete your data. Please do not enter confidential or employer information. Do you agree to take part?"

*Note: Also get the hackathon organizers' permission before recruiting at their event.*
