# Pilot Prompts & Answer Sheet Specification

## Condition A (Ordinary AI Baseline)
**First Message:**
```text
Help me understand this challenge, choose an idea, and get ready to present it.
```
*(Use the same model family as the other conditions where practical, e.g. Gemini 2.5 Flash / Flash Lite).*

---

## Condition B (Socratic AI Coach Baseline)
**First System / Seed Message:**
```text
You are a Socratic thinking coach for a hackathon team. Your goal is that I can explain and defend my own idea without you. Do not give me ideas or solutions until I have written my own problem statement and at least two candidate ideas; if I ask earlier, give one hint instead. Ask one question at a time and keep replies under 120 words. When I state something as a fact, ask what evidence I have or label it an assumption. Challenge my favourite idea with one counterexample or alternative explanation. If I say I am stuck, or ask directly twice, give a short explanation or example, then ask me to critique it. At the end, ask me to state the problem, my strongest evidence, my riskiest assumption, why this idea over an alternative, and what would change my mind.
```

---

## Anonymous Answer Sheet (Google Form Specification)
*Completed by participant after closing the AI tab (8-minute timer).*

### Form Fields:
1. **Participant Code** (e.g. P1 - P8)
2. **Session ID** (random 4 letters generated by facilitator; mapping to condition stays in private log)
3. **Problem Statement**: The problem and why it matters
4. **Strongest Evidence**: Strongest evidence gathered/supported
5. **Remaining Assumptions**: What remains an assumption
6. **Defense**: Why this idea over an alternative
7. **Falsifiability**: What evidence would change your mind
8. **Summary**: The idea in three sentences

*The scorer sees ONLY fields 2 through 8 (anonymized session ID + responses).*

---

## Private Session Log Columns
- `session_id`: Random 4-letter identifier
- `participant`: P1..P8
- `condition`: `thinkmate` | `socratic` | `ordinary`
- `brief`: X | Y
- `order`: 1 | 2
- `start`: Timestamp
- `end`: Timestamp
- `minutes_to_frame`: Minutes taken to reach confirmed frame
- `direct_answer_requests`: Count of explicit direct requests
- `abandoned`: y / n (and reason)
- `would_use`: y / n
- `situation`: Named situation where ThinkMate is preferred
- `notes`: Qualitative observations
